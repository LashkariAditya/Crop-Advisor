Privacy and data use

CropAdvisor is a research prototype. It has no user accounts and does not intentionally save uploaded images or field values. The image is processed in memory by the application. Do not enter personal or sensitive information.

When you request field context, latitude and longitude are sent to Open-Meteo for weather data and ISRIC SoilGrids for soil data. The optional climate endpoints also send coordinates to Open-Meteo's archive service or NASA POWER. These are third-party services with their own terms and privacy practices. CropAdvisor does not send the uploaded image to those services. In the public build, returned API context is cached in server memory only and is not written to disk.

The public demonstration uses experimental models and may be hosted by a third-party platform. That platform may process connection metadata and operational logs. Before any public launch, the maintainer must review the host's retention settings and publish a privacy notice that matches the chosen host and applicable requirements.

Product prices shown in the demo are mock data. Crop suggestions and economic estimates are not guarantees and should be checked with local agricultural experts.
